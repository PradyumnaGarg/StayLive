Made for macOS

Small app. Lots of care.

Built-in wallpapers

Aurora, Color Flow, Starfield and Dusk Waves are generated right on your Mac, ready the moment you open the app.

Drag in any video

Drop clips onto the window to add them to your library, with hover previews and a details panel.

Automatic conversion

Lock-screen videos are converted to the 4K · 240 fps format macOS expects, in a background queue with a time estimate.

Sound, done right

Audio plays from your main display only, so there's no echo across monitors, and fades out the moment the screen saver ends.

Easy on battery

Playback pauses when nobody can see it: covered desktop, locked or sleeping screen, and optionally on battery.

Lives in your menu bar

Play, pause, switch wallpapers and set the volume from the menu bar. Starts at login, so your desktop survives a restart.

Safe by design

Nothing hidden. Nothing risky.

StayLive only works with files in your own user Library. It leaves Apple's Aerials untouched, and one button undoes everything it adds.

Get started

Up and running in a minute.

Download

Get the latest StayLive.dmg from GitHub Releases and open it.

Drag to Applications

Drop StayLive onto the Applications folder in the window that appears.

Open it

StayLive isn't notarized by Apple, so the first time go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

Requires a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is StayLive really free?

Yes. No trial, no subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads. It's MIT-licensed open source, built as a personal project.

Why does macOS warn me the first time I open it?

Apple asks developers to pay for notarization. StayLive is a free hobby project and isn't notarized, so macOS asks you to confirm once in System Settings → Privacy & Security. If you'd rather not trust a download, build it from the source in a couple of commands.

Does the lock screen play sound?

No. Apple's lock-screen player has no audio support. Sound is available on the desktop and in the screen saver.

Will it drain my battery?

Videos are decoded by your Mac's media engine, and playback stops whenever the desktop isn't visible. You can also pause it automatically on battery.

How do I remove it?

In StayLive's Settings click Stop Using StayLive Aerials, pick another screen saver, then quit and delete the app. Full steps.

How does the lock screen trick work?

macOS plays its Aerial wallpapers from video files in your Library. StayLive converts your video to the same format and adds it to its own copy of the Aerial list. Read the technical write-up.
