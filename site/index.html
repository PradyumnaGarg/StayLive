(09) FAQ

Questions,
answered.

01

Is StayLive really free?

Yes. No trial, no subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads. It's MIT-licensed open source, built as a personal project.

02

Why does macOS warn me the first time?

Notarization is a paid Apple program and StayLive is a free hobby project, so macOS asks you to confirm once in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Prefer not to trust a download? Build it from source in two commands.

03

Does the lock screen play sound?

No. Apple's lock-screen player has no audio support. Sound works on the desktop and in the screen saver.

04

Will it drain my battery?

Videos are decoded by your Mac's media engine, and playback stops whenever the desktop isn't visible. You can also pause it automatically on battery.

05

How does the lock screen work?

macOS plays its Aerial wallpapers from video files in your Library. StayLive converts your video to the same format and adds it to its own copy of the Aerial list. Read the technical write-up.

06

How do I remove it?

In StayLive's Settings click Stop Using StayLive Aerials, pick another screen saver, then quit and delete the app. Full steps.
